# Contributing to Travel Companion

Thank you for helping make travel easier and more joyful for more people.

Every contribution must uphold the four principles:

- **Respect Travel:** lower the barriers to travel; do not turn a journey into a productivity contest.
- **Respect the Agent:** add capability without replacing or hijacking the user's existing Agent.
- **Respect Memory:** keep personal memories under the traveler's control; use consent and protect privacy.
- **Respect People:** keep recommendations independent of advertising, commissions, sponsorship and sales funnels.

Read the [Project Charter](CHARTER.md) before proposing a feature or submitting a change. These principles guide the skill, community contributions, partnerships and future commercial work.

## What we welcome

- Improvements for different planning approaches: chronological, structural, associative and mixed.
- Better handling of budget, language, accessibility, transport and time constraints.
- Clearer distinction between ideas, decisions, bookings and real experiences.
- Better post-trip reflection and traveler-controlled journals.
- Translations and platform-specific installation guidance that do not claim unsupported compatibility.
- Tests and examples using fictional or synthetic travel data.

## What we will not accept

- Advertising, paid placement, affiliate ranking or sales-funnel behavior.
- Changes that prioritize a sponsor, hotel, tour operator, restaurant or product because of a commercial relationship.
- Persona replacement or instructions that hijack the host Agent.
- Collection, publication or sharing of private traveler memories without explicit permission.
- Fabricated bookings, prices, availability, experiences or capabilities.
- Personal itineraries, booking references, identity documents, contact details, private photos or other sensitive information in public issues or pull requests.

## Contributor rights

- You retain copyright in your original contribution.
- Do not submit material you do not have the right to share.
- A pull request does not automatically transfer your copyright to the project owner.
- A contribution is not automatically authorized for inclusion in a future paid product. Separate, explicit permission is required for commercial reuse of third-party contributions.
- Before a contribution is merged, its applicable community license must be clear. The repository's general license is still being finalized; until then, use Issues for proposals or discuss licensing before submitting substantial reusable material.

## How to contribute

1. Open an Issue for a proposal, bug report or discussion.
2. Explain the traveler problem and who benefits; avoid starting with a feature for its own sake.
3. Describe how the change respects the four principles and what trade-offs it introduces.
4. Use synthetic examples. Never include real people's private travel records.
5. Keep changes focused and explain platform-specific assumptions.

## Review principles

A contribution should make the skill more useful without making it more controlling. We value clarity, portability, truthful capability boundaries, user choice and small understandable improvements over unnecessary complexity.
