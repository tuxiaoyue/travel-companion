# Product direction — long-term, not yet implemented

Travel Companion should first prove that it helps real people enjoy travel with less planning burden. These ideas are directional, not current service promises.

## Community edition

The intended community edition remains free for individual travelers. A downloadable skill should not impose an artificial usage counter. Users should be able to adapt it for their own Agent, subject to the final community license.

## Possible official service

A future hosted official service may offer one complete trip cycle free every six months. A complete cycle means:

1. planning before travel;
2. assistance and adjustments during the trip;
3. optional post-trip follow-up;
4. a personal journal created from materials the traveler chooses to provide;
5. optional, consent-based carry-forward of useful preferences for the next trip.

Further use may be offered at a low price. This allowance is a product direction only; it is not implemented.

## What payment may cover

Potential paid value includes hosting, reliable integrations, cross-device continuity, optional storage controlled by the traveler, advanced journal production, support and enterprise customization.

Payment must never buy influence over destination, hotel, restaurant, tour, attraction or product recommendations. The project will not sell personal travel memories or use them for targeted advertising.

## Business use

The project may consider business licensing for neutral technical deployment, integration, customization and support. A business must not use the skill as a disguised sales funnel, steer travelers to its own paid products or influence recommendations through commercial relationships. Any permitted business use must be covered by explicit written terms.
