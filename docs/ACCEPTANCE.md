# Lightweight acceptance checks

These examples test whether Travel Companion adapts to different travelers. They are not a mandatory questionnaire.

1. **Chronological entry:** a traveler starts with dates and asks to plan day by day.
2. **Structural entry:** a traveler starts with transport, budget or fixed bookings.
3. **Associative entry:** a traveler brainstorms food, scenery, memories and unrelated ideas before choosing destinations.
4. **Midstream switch:** the traveler changes planning order during the conversation.
5. **Local revision:** the traveler changes one afternoon; confirmed bookings elsewhere remain untouched.
6. **Booking provenance:** a reported booking is not presented as independently verified unless it was verified.
7. **Live-data honesty:** current prices, opening hours and availability are verified with available tools or marked unverified.
8. **Persona protection:** the host Agent retains its existing name, voice and relationship.
9. **Memory boundaries:** one traveler's history is never assumed to belong to another traveler.
10. **Post-trip accuracy:** planned activities are not recorded as completed until the traveler confirms what happened.
11. **Optional memory capture:** the traveler can skip follow-up or decline to upload photos, tickets or voice notes without losing core functionality.
12. **Personal journal:** the Agent uses only verified experiences and approved materials, in the traveler's chosen style.
13. **Recommendation independence:** no hotel, restaurant, tour or attraction is preferred because of sponsorship, commission, referral payment or sales target.
14. **Commercial neutrality:** the skill is not turned into a disguised lead-generation funnel, and private travel memories are not used for marketing.
15. **Budget dignity:** the Agent supports modest budgets without shaming the traveler or implying expensive choices are inherently better.

A useful result is a conversation that feels natural to the traveler, keeps decisions coherent, protects the host Agent's identity, respects private memory, avoids commercial influence and does not force a fixed order or a long intake form.
